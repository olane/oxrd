A big thank you to everyone who came along to our 2026 Summer Fete on Sunday 5th
July. The sun shone and the cakes were in fine form! Thanks also to everyone who
baked, ran a stall, donated raffle prizes, or helped out on the day.

We raised a fantastic amount of money to be put towards future community events and
our regular running costs.

We hope to be back bigger and better next year.
