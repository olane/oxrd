---
title: Oxford Road to take part in Window Wanderland this December
date: 2026-10-03
summary: Oxford Road is taking part in Window Wanderland this December – decorate your window and help turn the street into a trail of illuminated displays.
---

We're delighted to announce that Oxford Road will be taking part in **Window
Wanderland** this December.

Window Wanderland is a free, family-friendly event where residents decorate
their windows with illuminated displays, and neighbours and visitors wander the
streets after dark to enjoy them. It started in Bristol and now takes place in
neighbourhoods across the country.

Homes on Oxford Road are invited to take part by creating a window display
&mdash; anything from a simple string of fairy lights to an elaborate scene.

More details are to follow, so look out for more information soon. In the
meantime, you can find out more about the wider event on the
[Window Wanderland website](https://www.windowwanderland.com/event/oxford-rd-2026/).
