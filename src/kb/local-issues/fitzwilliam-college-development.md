# Fitzwilliam College development

Fitzwilliam College has been granted planning permission to redevelop its site
between 87&ndash;93 Oxford Road and its sportsground.

## The application

- **Reference:** 25/04484/FUL
- **Location:** 87&ndash;93 Oxford Road and Fitzwilliam College Sportsground
- **Summary:** Demolition of 87&ndash;89 Oxford Road and two stores, and
  construction of replacement student accommodation, part change of use and part
  refurbishment of the Red Cottage student accommodation, refurbishment and
  extension of the pavilion, a new squash court and groundskeepers store, and
  improvement of the access.

The application was approved at planning committee on 2 September 2026.

## What happens next

Approval comes with a set of conditions that the college must meet. The ORRA
committee will be working to make sure these are managed properly, including:

- managing noise and dust from the development
- a student management plan
- 24/7 contact arrangements for the college
- discharge of the conditions attached to the permission

We will post updates as things progress. If you have any questions or concerns,
email us at [hello@oxrd.co.uk](mailto:hello@oxrd.co.uk).
