# Your representatives

Oxford Road is represented at three levels of local and national government:

- **Cambridge City Council** — Castle ward
- **Cambridgeshire County Council** — Castle division
- **Parliament** — Cambridge constituency

Councillors and MPs are elected to help constituents, whatever your political
views. It's always worth getting in touch about issues that matter to you.

## Cambridge City Council (Castle ward)

Castle ward has three councillors on Cambridge City Council. The city council
is responsible for things like planning, housing, parks and play spaces, bins
and street cleanliness.
