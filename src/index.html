Welcome to the Oxford Road Residents Association (ORRA). We are a community group dedicated to improving and maintaining the quality of life on Oxford Road and the surrounding area.

If you live on Oxford Road and would like to get involved, please email us at hello@oxrd.co.uk.

We also have a street WhatsApp group and email list, either of which you are very welcome to join. To be added to either, just email us.
